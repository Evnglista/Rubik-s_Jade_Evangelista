About the Rubik's Cube

The Rubik's Cube is a popular 3D puzzle invented by Ernő Rubik in 1974. It challenges players to arrange each side into a single color.

Solving a cube can improve problem-solving skills, patience, and logical thinking.

A standard cube has 6 faces and 54 colored stickers. It is a fun activity for people of different ages.

Learn more from the World Cube Association .

Keep practicing and never give up!

Basic Cube Guide

Here are the six colors commonly found on a standard cube:

  • White — opposite yellow
  • Yellow — opposite white
  • Red — opposite orange
  • Orange — opposite red
  • Blue — opposite green
  • Green — opposite blue

Beginner Solving Steps

  1. Make the white cross.
  2. Solve the white corners.
  3. Complete the middle layer.
  4. Make the yellow cross.
  5. Finish the yellow face and last layer.

Tip: Practice a little every day to improve your solving speed.
